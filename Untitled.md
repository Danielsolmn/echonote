## New App Ideas – Evaluate and Categorize

### 1. AI Note Taker
**Description:** Capture meeting audio or typed notes, then automatically generate organized, searchable transcripts with AI-powered summaries, action items, and keyword highlights.  
**Category:** Productivity / Utilities  
**Mobile:** Uses microphone for audio capture and camera for optional whiteboard scans; combines on-device speech-to-text with open AI for summarization.  
**Story:** Press record in a session and by the end get a clean set of bullet points, decisions, and assigned tasks all searchable.  
**Market:** Students, professionals, meeting facilitators, and corporate teams.  
**Habit:** Users record or type during classes, calls, or workshops; review AI summaries immediately to stay aligned.  
**Scope:**  
- V1: Audio recording + raw transcript + basic summary  
- V2: Action-item extraction, tagging, export to calendar  
- V3: Live captions, speaker diarization  
- V4: Collaboration and integrations  

---

### 2. Face Detector & Emoji Display App
**Description:** A camera app that recognizes facial expressions in real time and overlays a matching emoji for selfies or videos.  
**Category:** Entertainment / Photo & Video  
**Mobile:** Uses front-facing camera and Vision APIs; Core ML model for expression classification.  
**Story:** Make a face and see the emoji mirror your expression live.  
**Market:** Social media users, content creators, kids and teens.  
**Habit:** Quick, repeatable fun used for selfies and reaction videos.  
**Scope:**  
- V1: Four basic expressions (happy, sad, surprised, neutral)  
- V2: Custom stickers and animations  
- V3: Video recording with emoji overlays  
- V4: Integration with Instagram/Snapchat  

---

### 3. AI Excel App
**Description:** Create, edit, and analyze spreadsheets on your phone using natural language or voice. Import data from photos of printed tables and run AI-powered queries like “Show me a bar chart of sales by region.”  
**Category:** Productivity / Business  
**Mobile:** Camera OCR for table import; voice or text input for commands; AI backend for chart creation and analysis.  
**Story:** Take a photo of a whiteboard table, import the data, and instantly get charts or insights without manual entry.  
**Market:** Business professionals, analysts, students, and small business owners.  
**Habit:** Daily data checks, quick meeting analysis, or on-the-go edits.  
**Scope:**  
- V1: Import CSV/Excel files, run basic queries  
- V2: Voice to formula, one tap chart creation  
- V3: Photo to sheet OCR  
- V4: Collaboration, cloud sync, AI macros  


# Final Idea: AI Note Taker
**Reason for Choice:**  
The AI Note Taker solves a common, high-value problem for students, professionals, and teams turning unstructured conversations into structured, searchable, and actionable notes. Its potential for daily use, combined with multiple upgrade paths (summarization, tagging, live captions, team collaboration), makes it both technically feasible and highly marketable.
